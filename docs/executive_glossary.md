\# Govern-X Executive Glossary



\## NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

A framework that helps organizations understand, manage, and communicate cybersecurity risk.



\## CSF Function

The main cybersecurity activities in the NIST CSF:

\- Identify – Understand cybersecurity risks.

\- Protect – Put safeguards in place.

\- Detect – Find cybersecurity events and weaknesses.

\- Respond – Take action when a cybersecurity incident occurs.

\- Recover – Restore operations and improve after an incident.



\## CSF Category

A more specific cybersecurity outcome within a CSF Function. Categories help organize security activities into practical areas.



\## CSF Subcategory

A detailed cybersecurity outcome that describes a specific security practice or result.



\## Implementation Tier

A measure of how an organization manages cybersecurity risk.



\### Tier 1 – Partial

Cybersecurity practices are limited and mostly reactive. Risk management may not be consistent across the organization.



\### Tier 2 – Risk Informed

The organization understands cybersecurity risks and uses some risk-based practices, but these practices may not be consistent across all areas.



\### Tier 3 – Repeatable

Cybersecurity practices are formally established, consistently followed, and regularly updated based on risk.



\### Tier 4 – Adaptive

Cybersecurity practices are continuously improved and adapted using lessons learned, threat information, and changing business needs.



\## Current Profile

A description of the cybersecurity outcomes an organization currently achieves.



\## Target Profile

A description of the cybersecurity outcomes an organization wants to achieve in the future.



\## Gap Analysis

A comparison between the Current Profile and Target Profile to identify areas that need improvement.



\## Govern-X

A cybersecurity compliance and risk assessment platform that evaluates security controls, maps results to the NIST CSF, and helps organizations understand their security gaps.
